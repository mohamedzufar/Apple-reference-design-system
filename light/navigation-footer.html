NAVIGATION + FOOTER SYSTEM

Thoughtful tools. Less friction.

A complete site-chrome reference: desktop capsule navigation, responsive mobile menu, grouped footer links, legal links, and copyright hierarchy.

Explore the system ↘Switch to dark theme

PAGE STRUCTURE

Navigation stays quiet.
Content leads.

Keep the global header predictable and let each page's primary message carry the visual weight.

01 / HEADER

Few primary destinations

Brand, 3–6 main links, optional sign-in, and one primary action. Secondary destinations belong in the footer.

02 / FOOTER

Organized, not crowded

Product, resources, company, and legal groups, followed by a clear copyright and policy row.

LINK HIERARCHY

Footer link groups

Use only destinations that exist and policies the company can accurately stand behind.

Product

Overview, Features, Pricing, Changelog

Resources

Documentation, Guides, Help center, Contact

Company & legal

About, Privacy Policy, Terms, Cookie preferences where applicable

LINK BEHAVIOR

Clear in every state.

Hover changes contrast, keyboard focus remains visible, and mobile links get comfortable tap spacing. No legal link should depend on hover to be discoverable.

Make the structure feel effortless.

One clear navigation system. One footer hierarchy. Consistent behavior across every page.
